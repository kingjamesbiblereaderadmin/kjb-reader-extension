WHAT'S NEW IN v0.4.272 (September 23, 2026)

- Bible-reference detection is allowed on kingjamesbiblereader.com/extension for testing, but stays disabled on all other pages of that site. The exemption is limited to the exact page (with or without a trailing slash), not pages such as /extension-privacy.
- Includes recent search fixes: apostrophes and Æ ligatures work together ("Caesar's" finds "Cæsar’s"), and long searches no longer lose verses when Psalm titles or other structural lines appear.
