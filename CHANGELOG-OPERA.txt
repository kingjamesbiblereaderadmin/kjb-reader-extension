WHAT'S NEW IN v0.4.259 (September 21, 2026)

- Fixed searches that combine the Æ ligature with an apostrophe, like "Caesar's" — the Bible prints "Cæsar’s", which previously returned no results.
- Fixed long keyword searches silently dropping verses near the end of the results (Psalm titles and other structural lines were taking up result slots), e.g. "right" was missing Revelation 22:14.
